Veg Sandwich Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 Bread Slices
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 Tomato (sliced)
  • 1 Onion (sliced)
  • 1 Cucumber (sliced)
  • 2 Cheese Slices
  • Salt to taste
  • Black Pepper

Preparation Steps

  1. Spread butter evenly on all bread slices.
  2. Arrange tomato, onion, and cucumber slices on one bread slice.
  3. Sprinkle salt and black pepper over the vegetables.
  4. Place a cheese slice on top of the vegetables.
  5. Cover with another bread slice.
  6. Toast the sandwich in a toaster or pan until golden brown.
  7. Cut the sandwich into halves.
  8. Serve hot with ketchup or green chutney.
